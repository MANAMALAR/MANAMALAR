FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We have answers. Here's everything you need to know before starting your mental wellness journey with us.

What is the difference between a Psychologist and a Psychiatrist?

A Psychologist provides therapy and counselling using evidence-based psychological techniques such as CBT, DBT, and mindfulness. A Psychiatrist is a medical doctor who can prescribe medication for mental health conditions. At Manamalar, our Clinical Psychologist offers comprehensive psychological assessment and therapy, and will refer to a psychiatrist when medication management is also needed.

Are my sessions completely confidential?

Absolutely. Everything discussed in your sessions is strictly confidential and protected by professional ethics. Your information will never be shared with family, employers, or any third party without your explicit written consent. The only exceptions are situations involving immediate risk to life.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies based on the nature and severity of your concern, your personal goals, and your progress. Some issues can be addressed in 6-8 sessions, while others may benefit from longer-term support. Your therapist will discuss a realistic timeline after the initial assessment and review it regularly as you progress.

What languages are sessions available in?

Counselling sessions are available in Tamil, English, Malayalam, and Hindi — language is never a barrier here. Our specialists speak: Dr. Azhagannan. K — Tamil, English & Hindi; K. Sa. Murugan — Tamil, English, Hindi & Malayalam; R. Nithiya Sruthi — Tamil & English. You are welcome to communicate in whichever language makes you most comfortable.

When can I book an appointment?

Appointments are available every day from Monday through Sunday. The clinic is closed on Sundays, so Sunday bookings are accepted for appointment requests only and do not include online or offline sessions. You can book in advance by filling out the appointment form, calling us at 9940879221 or 73059208791, or messaging us on WhatsApp at 9487413221.

What are the clinic and online consultation timings?

Both clinic visits and online consultations run from 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Sessions are not held on Sundays. Phone and WhatsApp enquiries are taken every day from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

What should I expect in my first session?

Your first session is primarily an assessment and getting-to-know-you conversation. Your therapist will ask about your background, current concerns, symptoms, and what you hope to achieve through therapy. There is no pressure - you share only what you are comfortable with. By the end of the session, you will have a clearer idea of the treatment approach best suited for you.
